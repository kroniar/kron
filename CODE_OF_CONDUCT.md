# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to fostering a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.

## Our Standards
Positive behavior includes:
- Using inclusive and respectful language
- Being empathetic and collaborative
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment or discrimination
- Trolling or personal attacks
- Publishing private information

## Enforcement
Report violations to: **contact@kroniar.dev** (replace with your email).  
Maintainers will review and respond appropriately.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
